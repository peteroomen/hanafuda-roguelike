# Aotearoa card faces: licence

A 48-card hanafuda deck redrawn with New Zealand plants, birds and skies, and the kiwi mark
(`src/ui/art/Kiwi.tsx`), made for Twelve Petals by Peter Oomen. The sources are the SVGs in
`art-source/cards/aotearoa/` (see the README there for how they were made). The files here are
converted by `scripts/aotearoa-cards.ts`: cropped at the sides to the game's card shape,
bordered, and saved as 320×525 WebP.

4.0 licence of the traditional faces, and may not be reused outside Twelve Petals without
permission.

The ribbon inscriptions are set in Yuji Boku (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and converted to
outlines; the font's licence covers the typeface, not these images.
