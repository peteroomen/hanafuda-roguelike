# Aotearoa card faces

A 48-card hanafuda deck redrawn with New Zealand plants, birds and skies, made for Twelve Petals.
The sources are the SVGs in `art-source/cards/aotearoa/` (see the README there for how they were
made). The files here are converted by `scripts/aotearoa-cards.ts`: cropped at the sides to the
game's card shape, bordered, and saved as 320×525 WebP.

The ribbon inscriptions are set in Yuji Boku (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and converted to
outlines.
