# Subject prompts for the remaining spirits

Put each subject block after the style and composition blocks in `README.md`. For a clean
cutout, keep the background plain and untextured, and close the outlines wherever the
figure meets it. Bosses get a gold frame in the game, so give them bigger, more commanding
poses.

## Spring

### zashikiWarashi

```
SUBJECT: Zashiki-warashi, "The House Child"
A zashiki-warashi, a mischievous child spirit whose favoured houses prosper. A small
child of about five with a neat black bob haircut with straight bangs, rosy cheeks
and a cheeky gap-toothed grin, wearing a red kimono with a small white flower
pattern. She holds up a string of old square-holed gold coins (mon) in one hand as a
prize and hides a spinning top behind her back with the other. Playful and a little
smug, like she knows she'll win.
```

## Summer

### chochinObake

```
SUBJECT: Chōchin-obake, "The Lantern Tongue"
A chōchin-obake, an old paper lantern come alive. A ribbed, oval paper lantern with a
black lacquered cap and base. The paper has split open into a wide grinning mouth,
with one big round eye above it, and a long red tongue curls out hungrily. A warm
orange glow shines through the paper from inside. It stretches its tongue towards a
small bright sun disc floating nearby, wanting every bright thing. Greedy and
giddy.
```

### kawauso

```
SUBJECT: Kawauso, "The River Otter"
A kawauso, a shape-shifting river otter that loves a riverside wager. A sleek brown
otter standing upright on a mossy river rock, with a wet shiny coat, round whiskered
face and small dark eyes. A folded straw hat sits on its head and a small drawstring
purse of coins hangs from its neck, and it fans out three hanafuda cards in one paw
like a card sharp. A curl of river water with a few leaves swirls around its feet.
Friendly-looking, sly underneath.
```

### hitotsumeKozo

```
SUBJECT: Hitotsume-kozō, "The One-Eyed Boy"
A hitotsume-kozō, a bald little monk boy with one enormous eye. A small round shaved
head, a single huge round eye in the middle of his face with a tiny dark pupil
looking straight at the viewer, and a small cheeky smile. He wears a plain grey
novice monk's robe and holds a hanafuda card up close to his eye, peering at it,
because he sees everyone's cards. Curious and a bit of a know-it-all.
```

### kappa (boss)

```
SUBJECT: Kappa, "The River Imp" (a boss)
A kappa, a river imp with a dish of water on its head. A green, frog-like, child-sized
creature with a turtle shell on its back, a duck-like yellow beak, webbed hands and a
ring of shaggy dark hair around a shallow dish on top of its head, brimming with
water. It stands in a swirl of river water, gleefully tearing a red paper ribbon strip
in half, with other torn ribbons floating away in the current. Its eyes are wide and
triumphant. Cheeky and bossy.
```

### namazu (boss)

```
SUBJECT: Ōnamazu, "The Earthquake Catfish" (a boss)
Ōnamazu, a giant catfish that lives under the islands and shakes the earth when it
thrashes. A huge dark indigo-grey catfish curling through the frame, with long
drooping whiskers, a wide grumpy mouth, and small gold eyes. Its body is thrashing,
and around it a few tiles and hanafuda cards are tumbling into the air. Short curved
motion lines around its body show the ground shaking. Enormous, sulky and powerful.
```

## Autumn

### bakeneko

```
SUBJECT: Bakeneko, "The Changeling Cat"
A bakeneko, a house cat grown old enough to walk upright and hold a grudge. A
calico cat (white with orange and black patches) standing on its hind legs, wearing
a small indigo kimono jacket. Its long tail splits into two at the tip, and a
flicker of blue ghost-fire floats above one paw. Half-closed eyes and a smug little
smile, with one paw resting on a stolen fish. Haughty and covetous.
```

### ittanMomen

```
SUBJECT: Ittan-momen, "The Flying Cloth"
An ittan-momen, a long strip of white cotton cloth that flutters down on travellers
at dusk. A single long ribbon of off-white cloth curling and looping through the air
in a big S-shape, with a simple face near the top end: two narrow slit eyes and a
thin mischievous smile. Its tail end frays into several red and indigo paper ribbon
strips (tanzaku), as if it has been collecting them. A few autumn maple leaves
tumble around it. Floaty and sneaky.
```

### nopperabo

```
SUBJECT: Nopperabō, "The Faceless One"
A nopperabō, a faceless yokai that looks like anyone at all until it turns to face
you. A figure in a plain muted-green travelling kimono and a wide straw hat, shown
from the chest up and turning towards the viewer. Where its face should be is
perfectly smooth blank skin, like an egg, with no features at all. It holds one
hanafuda card face-down in front of it. The mood is eerie but still charming: a
quiet, polite stranger with no face.
```

### kitsune (boss)

```
SUBJECT: Kitsune, "The Nine-Tailed Illusion" (a boss)
A kitsune, an ancient fox whose foxfire makes things seem what they are not. A white
fox with red markings around its eyes and on its ears, sitting upright and elegant,
with nine big tails fanned out behind it like a peacock's display. Small floating
blue-white flames of foxfire drift around it, and one flame is turning a hanafuda
card into a different card. Narrow, knowing eyes and a faint smile. Beautiful,
mysterious and a little dangerous.
```

### nue (boss)

```
SUBJECT: Nue, "The Chimera of the Palace Roof" (a boss)
A nue, a chimera perched on a palace roof ridge at night: a red-faced monkey's head
with a clever, calculating stare; a round badger body; striped tiger legs gripping
the roof tiles; and a green serpent for a tail, curling up with its own small head.
A thin crescent moon sits behind it. It holds up one clawed finger as if taking notes
on your tricks. Watchful and smart.
```

## Winter

### kamaitachi

```
SUBJECT: Kamaitachi, "The Sickle Weasel"
A kamaitachi, a weasel riding the winter wind with sickles for claws. A slim, quick
weasel with ochre and white fur, mid-leap on a swirl of wind drawn as curling white
lines with a few snowflakes. Its front paws end in small curved silver sickle blades,
it has a sharp fox-like grin, and its eyes are narrowed and eager. Fast, sharp and
impatient.
```

### rokurokubi

```
SUBJECT: Rokurokubi, "The Long Neck"
A rokurokubi, a woman whose neck stretches at night. An elegant woman in a dark
indigo kimono with a white collar, with glossy black hair in a traditional bun held by
a red comb. Her neck stretches long and curves in a loop so her head can peer
sideways at the viewer with a sly, knowing smile, and she dangles a hanafuda card
from her teeth. Keep the neck a clean, simple curve that stays inside the frame.
Graceful and teasing, not grotesque.
```

### yamauba

```
SUBJECT: Yama-uba, "The Mountain Crone"
A yama-uba, a crone of the high passes who shelters travellers and then eats them. An
old woman with wild long white hair, bright eyes and a wide welcoming grin that
shows a couple of pointed teeth. She wears a patched brown and ochre mountain kimono
with a straw rain cape over her shoulders. She holds out a steaming cup of tea in one
hand, and behind her back the other hand holds a big wooden ladle. Cosy on the
surface, a little too hungry underneath.
```

### yukiOnna (boss)

```
SUBJECT: Yuki-onna, "The Snow Woman" (a boss)
Yuki-onna, a snow woman who walks in on the last night of the year. A tall, pale
woman with very long straight black hair, in a pure white kimono with a pale blue
snowflake pattern, and pale blue lips. She holds one hand out towards the viewer,
and frost crystals spread from her fingertips over a hanafuda card, freezing it in
ice. Snowflakes drift around her. Serene, beautiful and cold, with a small calm
smile.
```

### oni (final boss)

```
SUBJECT: Oni, "The Ogre at the Gate" (the final boss)
An oni, a red ogre with an iron club who collects debts on the last night of the
year. A big, muscular red ogre with two short horns, wild black hair, bulging gold
eyes and a wide fanged grin. He wears a tiger-skin loincloth and has a heavy studded
iron club (kanabō) resting on one shoulder. With his other hand he holds out a long
paper ledger, demanding payment. Huge and intimidating, but still in the playful,
bold style of the other characters, not horror.
```
